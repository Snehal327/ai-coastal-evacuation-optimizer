# AI-Powered Last-Mile Evacuation Optimization for Coastal Maharashtra

## Track
Track 5 - Cyclone Impact & Infrastructure Vulnerability Forecaster

## Problem Statement

Cyclones can cause severe damage to coastal communities and infrastructure.
Traditional evacuation planning may not dynamically account for cyclone
trajectory, building vulnerability, road conditions, and population distribution.

## Proposed Solution

We propose an AI-powered evacuation decision-support system for coastal
Maharashtra that combines:

- Cyclone path prediction
- Building vulnerability assessment
- Population-risk estimation
- Evacuation route optimization
- Real-time emergency alerts

The system aims to help authorities identify high-risk areas, prioritize
evacuations, and provide safer and faster evacuation routes.

## Key Modules

1. Cyclone Prediction
   - Analyze historical and current cyclone data
   - Estimate potential cyclone impact zones

2. Infrastructure Vulnerability
   - Assess buildings based on structural and geographic factors
   - Generate vulnerability scores

3. Risk Mapping
   - Combine cyclone intensity, location, population, and infrastructure risk
   - Generate high-risk zones

4. Evacuation Route Optimization
   - Identify safe evacuation centers
   - Calculate efficient routes
   - Consider road distance and risk levels

5. Emergency Alerts
   - Provide location-based evacuation warnings
   - Prioritize high-risk areas
